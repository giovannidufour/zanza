# 0168 — Genre and tag pages: the whole catalog as a checklist

`2026-11-24` · proposed (Gio: "vorrei in admin nella pagina dettaglio attribute sia genre che tag la lista del catalogo con dei checkbox, per vedere dove è assegnato e assegnare l'attribute velocemente") · amends 0034 · Figma: BACKEND → Attributes → `Genre - desktop · edit · records column` (2523:1736, 1440 wide), rows = COMPONENTS → `admin` → `listing row` (2299:1910, handle off, price off)

**Context.** A genre or tag page listed only the records that already had it, as plain links. Giving a genre to ten records meant opening ten listings.
**Decision.** On a genre or tag page (not on artists and labels: a credit is not a yes/no) the Records section lists every copy in the catalog except archived ones, as `ListingRow`s with a checkbox, thumbnail, title link to the edit page and meta "SKU · status · section", on desktop and mobile. Checked = the copy's release has the attribute. A tick saves at once (no Save): insert into / delete from `release_genres` or `release_tags` (admin RLS), a new one goes last in the release's list; genres then refresh `releases.genres` (`sync_genres`); when a copy of that release is not a draft the shop rebuild is requested (`mark_site_build`). A toast confirms; on error the box goes back and the toast says why. Genres and tags belong to the release, so copies of the same release tick together and the toast says so. Order: the copies that have it first (as loaded, rows never move while ticking), then the shop order (0129). A search field filters by record name or SKU. The heading count "Records N" and the Delete rule follow the ticks. No migration.
**Layout (Gio 2026-11-24, after merge: "preferirei vederlo in una colonna a sé stante sulla destra").** The checklist is a column of its own on the right: the page goes full width, form 5 · actions 3 · records 4 of the 12 columns. The column sticks to the top of the viewport and its list scrolls inside it, so the heading and search stay in view. Mobile: it follows the form (after Merge or split).
**Rule.** A yes/no relation between one attribute and many records is edited where the attribute lives, as a checklist of the records that saves on each tick; never a "pick records" dialog.
**Where.** `apps/admin/admin.js` (`RecordChecklist`, `KINDS` `links` / `col`, `AttributeForm`), `apps/admin/admin.css` (`.admin-page--records`, `.admin-records`).
