# 0204 — Create bargain: side A, side B, one check, Save & next; camera on for the whole session; Live or Phone camera

`2026-11-27` · proposed · replaces the screens and the "one photo = one draft" step of 0203 · Figma: page `Crate photo` → components `capture slot` (2621:949), `capture progress` (2621:950), `label review` (2621:963); screens `Create bargain - side A` (2622:957), `- side B` (2622:1024), `- check and save` (2622:1142), `- next record` (2622:1312), `- phone camera (no viewer)` (2623:1040); notes 2612:5477

**Context.** Gio's first test of 0203 on the phone (2026-11-27): (1) the viewfinder was taller than the screen, he had to scroll to see what he framed; (2) the toast covered a lot of the screen; (3) Chrome asked for the camera at every record (the camera was restarted each time); (4) a bargain needs both labels, and one shot = one draft forced a bad flow. Then: "could Open camera open the native camera? with the sheet under the record the guides are not needed".
**Decision.**
- **One record = three steps**: side A → side B (or `No side B`) → check and save. The step is shown by `capture progress` (two `capture slot`s, now / taken / next / skipped); a taken slot is a button = retake that side. Head line: "Record N · 7″ · side A | side B | check and save".
- **Check and save**: both labels side by side (`label review`), each turned on its own (drag, arrow keys, 90°), Retake either; only warning checks are listed, prefixed "Label A:" / "Label B:". `Save & next` | `Discard` (back to side A, nothing saved).
- **Save & next** makes the photos (1600) and returns at once to side A of record N+1; uploads + draft run in the background, one record after the other: Label A → photo 1, Label B → photo 2, both Fulls → `original_keys`, draft as 0203 ("Untitled bargain", 7″, For sale, `bargain`). Up to 5 tries (3 s, 6 s, …), files already uploaded are not sent again.
- **No toasts on this page**: the note under the slots says "Saving record N…", "Record N saved · SKU 48213 · in Drafts", "Record N not saved yet: trying again"; camera messages go in the viewer pill (Phone camera: a warning check line above the slots).
- **Camera on for the whole session**: getUserMedia once per page (Take photo too: it stays on behind the result screen), so the browser asks once per page load.
- **Viewfinder fits the screen**: on camera screens the page is exactly the viewport and the 3:4 viewer takes the height left (it is only a reference, smaller is fine).
- **Live | Phone camera** switch (remembered on the device, `localStorage hm-bargain-camera`): Live = viewer with guides + `Take photo`; Phone camera = no viewer, `Open camera` = the phone's own camera (`<input capture="environment">`): better focus and steadiness, no permission prompt. Side B's secondary is `No side B`, otherwise `From library`.
**Rule.** A bargain record is always shot A then B and checked once before it is saved; saving never blocks the next shot; the capture page never shows toasts and never makes the user scroll to see the viewfinder.
**Open.** (1) Chrome/Safari may still ask for the camera at each page load (browser setting) · (2) ~~colour calibration against the Scanner~~ done in 0205 · (3) a record whose 5 tries fail is lost after leaving the page (its photos stay in R2).
**Where.** `apps/admin/admin.js` (`useCrateCamera`, `BargainCapture`, `CratePhoto` camera kept mounted), `apps/admin/admin.css` (`.admin-crate--camera`, `.admin-crate__frame`), `packages/ui` (`CaptureSlot`, `CaptureProgress`, `LabelReview`).
