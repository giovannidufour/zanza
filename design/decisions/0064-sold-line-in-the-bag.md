# 0064 — A sold copy in the bag: "Sold out · not in your order", price struck through, and a note above Checkout

`2026-11-12` · accepted (Gio, options A + A) · Figma: page `Bag` → `bag item` status=sold | reserved (was `unavailable`), `bag summary` boolean `sold out` + text `sold out note`, screens `Bag - desktop · just sold` / `Bag - mobile · just sold`

**Context.** A copy can sell while it sits in someone's bag. The line said "Just sold" in label/small with no price, which was a weak signal (design/open.md, Bag (4)). It also never said whether the record was still in the order. On cards and the PDP a sold copy is already a disabled "Sold out" Button with the label struck through (0061).
**Decision.** `bag item` `status` = available | sold | reserved. Sold: notice "Sold out · not in your order" (label/small) on top, and the price stays but is struck through (`<s>`). Remove is still the only action. Reserved keeps the line as it was (notice "Reserved", no price), because a hold is temporary and is not struck through (0059). The `bag summary` shows "1 record sold out, not included." / "N records sold out, not included." (body/small) above Checkout, but only while the bag has sold lines. Sold lines are left out of the subtotal, the shipping estimate and the record count. Checkout stays enabled for the rest of the bag. Screen readers get the words, not just the strikethrough.
**Rule.** Struck through means unavailable for good, on every surface (Button label, bag price). When something is left out of what the visitor pays for, say so in words, both on the item and next to the action that pays.
**Where.** `packages/ui/components.js` (`BagItem` status, `BagSummary` `soldOutNote` / `soldOutNoteProps`), `components.css`, `components.d.ts`; `apps/storefront/pages/bag.js`; `apps/storefront/scripts/site.js` (`syncBagLine`, `renderBagPage`).
