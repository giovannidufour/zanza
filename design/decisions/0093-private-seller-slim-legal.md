# 0093 — Private seller: slim legal pages, no Impressum or Withdrawal, a contact form

`2026-11-15` · accepted (merged on Gio's "merge" 2026-11-15; Gio: "sono un privato che vende saltuariamente e vorrei evitare tutte quelle cose legali lunghe"; slim texts by Claude, not legal advice) · Figma: page `Legal` → `contact form`, `Contact - desktop / mobile`, `Legal - desktop / mobile · Terms`; `footer legal` and `menu layer` without Impressum and Withdrawal

**Context.** 0079 wrote the legal pages for a trader (Unternehmer). Gio sells occasionally, as a private person, and does not want his home address or phone on the site.
**Decision.** Same template as 0079, shorter content: No Impressum, no address, no phone (Gio, 2026-11-15: "nessun indirizzo fisico, solo email e un contact form"): /contact/ = his email + a form (Name optional, Email, Message) sent by the Supabase function `contact` to his mailbox, nothing stored. Privacy = one screen (who, the services in one list, legal bases, rights). Terms = a few lines: private sale, used single copies as described, no withdrawal right and no warranty beyond the description, shipping, trades, German law. Shipping unchanged except returns. The Impressum and Withdrawal pages and their links (footer, mobile menu) are removed; the footer legal row is Privacy, Terms. Supersedes 0079's trader texts; 0079's template and Rule stay.
**Risk (accepted by Gio, to verify).** Whether he is private depends on how he sells (volume, regularity), not on the label: if a court sees a business, the missing withdrawal right and Impressum can lead to an Abmahnung (an Impressum needs a postal address where he can be served; a c/o service is the usual way to keep the home private).
**Rule.** Legal texts follow the seller status recorded here; if Gio's volume grows to regular sales, revisit 0079's trader texts.
