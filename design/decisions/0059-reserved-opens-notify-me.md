# 0059 — A reserved copy: its Add buttons read "Reserved" (secondary + clock), stay active and open the "Reserved" dialog with Notify me

`2026-11-12` · accepted (Gio, option A) · Figma: page `buttons` → `reserved (proposal)` (2208:397), page PDP → `reserved + notify me (proposal)` (2210:2636); no new Button variant

**Context.** Every copy is unique. A copy held during a checkout looked sold to everyone else (hidden from the PLP, nothing said why). Gio: the bag never holds; the hold lasts only while someone is in Stripe Checkout (max 30 min) and must read as a pause, explained to the visitor, with a way to be told when it is back.
**Decision.** A reserved copy stays in the PLP. Every Add button for it (card, PDP, player) reads "Reserved", hierarchy `secondary`, `leading icon` = `icon/icon-clock-counter-clockwise`, accessible name "Reserved, find out more". It stays active: pressing it opens the "Reserved" dialog (explanation + email field + Notify me → toast "We'll email you if it's back."). The PDP also shows the explanation in one body/small line under the CTA. The text depends on the hold: checkout ("back within 30 minutes") or manual ("on hold for someone"). The bag line shows the notice "Reserved". Reserved wins over "In bag". Notify me sends one email when the copy is available again and then deletes the address (migration 0016).
**Rule.** A temporary "can't right now" on a Button keeps it active, says the state in the label with a leading icon, and leads to the explanation and the next step (here: Notify me). `disabled` (struck through) stays for "unavailable for good".
