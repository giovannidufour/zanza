# 0120 — Home v2: welcome line, genres + view toggle, "This week" and "Recent" shelves around a newsletter box

`2026-11-18` · accepted (Gio: "procediamo con questa versione", then "builda e mergia") · replaces [0085](0085-home-page.md) · Figma: page `Home` → section `screens` (`Home - desktop` 2380:5789, `Home - mobile` 2388:7005, `note: home behaviour`), section `components` (`section head`, `genre links` layout row | column, `view toggle`, `home shelf`, `home intro`, `home newsletter`); page `product card` → `product tile`, `gallery counter`

**Context.** The 0085 home (Just in sale + trade, Genres, About) was a PLP with extras. Gio redrew it as a shop window: a welcome line, the week's batch first, the newsletter as the natural next step, then the rest. Trade and personal info are left out for now.
**Decision.**
- **Intro**: a visible welcome line under the header (body/extra-large desktop, body/large mobile; 16 above and below, 8 below on mobile) ending with an underlined "Subscribe to newsletter" that jumps to the newsletter box. The line is editable in the admin (Content → Short texts → Home, `short.home.intro`); the link is interface text.
- **Side (desktop, 3 of 12 columns)**: genres one per line as Tag size=large links (available sale genres, A–Z, → `/sale/?genre=`), then "All" (Button secondary extra-small, → /sale/), no title; below, the **view toggle** (secondary Button): "List view" switches both shelves to the PDP product cards, "Grid view" back to tiles. The choice is remembered in the visitor's browser. Mobile: genres wrap in rows, no toggle, always tiles.
- **Main (9 columns)**: shelf **This week (n)** = sale records (available or reserved) published in the last 7 days, newest first, max 6; `n` = all of them. Then the **newsletter box**: dashed like the store name, 16 around it, 2 of the 3 tile columns wide on desktop (full width on mobile), "Get new records first" + email field + Subscribe + "Get them in your inbox as they’re listed. Privacy". Then shelf **Recent** = the next 6 newest sale records. With nothing published this week, Recent comes first and the box after it. Both shelves: "Play all" (only the samples on screen) and "See all" → /sale/. The footer hides its own newsletter on the home (one signup per page).
- **Product tile** (grid view): desktop 3 per row (gap 8, rows 16): cover 1:1, name + release info, price + numbered samples, "Add to bag" fill (the same live states as every Add button: In bag, (Reserved) Notify me, Sold out). Mobile: one full-width tile per row (gap 16), every photo in a horizontal swipe gallery like the PDP, with a counter "1/3" (surface/accent) 8px from the bottom-left corner. Hover = surface/hover, pointer only.
- Section head: title body/medium, inline meta "(12)" text/moderate, gap small, top-aligned. `home about`, `Just in` and the trade mix of 0085 are gone; `/catalog/` stays built but is no longer linked from the home.
**Rule.** The home shows sale records only, newest first, in shelves of 6 (`home shelf`: section head + tiles | cards) with the newsletter box between the first two; a new home block is a `section head` + existing components. Tiles are for browsing (grid), cards for comparing (list); mobile is always tiles. Whatever a shelf renders twice (grid and list), behaviour must act on what is visible only.
**Where.** `packages/ui` (`SectionHead` meta, `GenreLinks` layout + All, `HomeIntro`, `HomeNewsletter`, `ProductTile`, `GalleryCounter`, `ViewToggle`, FooterNewsletter `hideLabel`; `docs/Home/`), `apps/storefront/pages/home.js`, `home.css`, `build.js` (footer without newsletter on /), `pages/shell.js` (SiteFooter `newsletter`, IMAGE_SIZES.tile), `data/products.js` (listing.published_at), `scripts/site.js` (view toggle, tile counter, Play all visible only), `packages/content/content.js` (`short.home`).

**Amended 2026-11-18 (Gio).** In a row of tiles every tile is as tall as the tallest one, and the price row and the button sit at its bottom: the buttons of a row line up whatever the title length (the price row stays attached to the button). Code: grid stretch + `margin-bottom: auto` on the title block.
