# 0130 — Privacy at the legal minimum: one bullet per processing

`2026-11-22` · accepted (merged on Gio's "merge") (Gio: "riduci il più possibile le informazioni fornite, il minimo indispensabile previsto dalla legge"; text by Claude, not legal advice) · Figma: page `Legal` → `note: legal pages` (no Privacy screen, same template as Terms)

**Context.** 0093 already slimmed Privacy for a private seller, but it still had prose, a "no tracking, no analytics" promise that analytics (parallel thread, PostHog cookieless) would make false, and no Account section (open.md → Account (10)).
**Decision.** Privacy keeps only what GDPR Art. 13 asks: controller and contact; per processing one bullet with the data, the provider, the legal basis and how long it is kept; recipients and US transfers with their safeguard in one line; whether giving data is required; the rights incl. withdrawing consent and the complaint (Art. 77). No marketing promises ("no cookies", "no analytics"), no explanations of how things work. Adds Account and the shop mailbox (Google) as a recipient of contact and trade messages. The coming-soon Privacy follows the same shape. A new processing (e.g. analytics) adds one bullet, nothing more.
Gio 2026-11-22, second pass: "riduci al minimo, sii più generico che puoi": providers named only by category (hosting, database, email, payments, statistics), no provider names; orders, account and Notify me in one bullet.
**Judgment calls (to check with a lawyer before launch).** Order retention says "as long as tax and civil law require" without a number (a private seller has no § 147 AO duty; civil claims ~3 years). Controller = no name, only "{{storeName}}, run by a private person" + email and contact form. Art. 13(1)(a) asks for the controller's identity, i.e. the full name of a natural person: this is NOT compliant to the letter. Gio 2026-11-22, after the risk was explained (complaint to the DPA, Abmahnung only if he counts as a trader): "non usare neanche Gio, corro il rischio". Accepted risk: revisit with 0093 if sales become regular. Bag and sign-in in the browser are named without a § 25 TDDDG section (strictly necessary). Recipients by category only (Art. 13(1)(e) allows it; a DPA or a reader may still ask which provider: answer by email).
**Rule.** Every new processing of personal data gets exactly one short bullet in `content/legal/privacy.md` (data, purpose, Art. 6 basis, how long), generic (no provider names); a new kind of provider → add its category to the providers line.
**Where.** `apps/storefront/content/legal/privacy.md`, `privacy-coming-soon.md`, `site.json` → legal.updated. If Privacy was saved in the admin (Content → Pages → Privacy), the saved text wins over the file: reset it there.
