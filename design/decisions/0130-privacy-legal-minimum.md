# 0130 — Privacy at the legal minimum: one bullet per processing

`2026-11-22` · proposed (Gio: "riduci il più possibile le informazioni fornite, il minimo indispensabile previsto dalla legge"; text by Claude, not legal advice)

**Context.** 0093 already slimmed Privacy for a private seller, but it still had prose, a "no tracking, no analytics" promise that analytics (parallel thread, PostHog cookieless) would make false, and no Account section (open.md → Account (10)).
**Decision.** Privacy keeps only what GDPR Art. 13 asks: controller and contact; per processing one bullet with the data, the provider, the legal basis and how long it is kept; recipients and US transfers with their safeguard in one line; whether giving data is required; the rights incl. withdrawing consent and the complaint (Art. 77). No marketing promises ("no cookies", "no analytics"), no explanations of how things work. Adds Account and the shop mailbox (Google) as a recipient of contact and trade messages. The coming-soon Privacy follows the same shape. A new processing (e.g. analytics) adds one bullet, nothing more.
**Judgment calls (to check with a lawyer before launch).** Order retention says "as long as tax and civil law require" without a number (a private seller has no § 147 AO duty; civil claims ~3 years). The controller still needs Gio's name (`site.json` → legal.name). Bag and sign-in in the browser are named without a § 25 TDDDG section (strictly necessary). Stripe (Ireland) is not listed as a transfer.
**Rule.** Every new processing of personal data gets exactly one bullet in `content/legal/privacy.md` (what, who, Art. 6 basis, how long); provider outside the EU → add it to the transfers line.
**Where.** `apps/storefront/content/legal/privacy.md`, `privacy-coming-soon.md`, `site.json` → legal.updated. If Privacy was saved in the admin (Content → Pages → Privacy), the saved text wins over the file: reset it there.
