# 0103 — Newsletter: our own double opt-in, signup in place, one click in the email

`2026-11-15` · accepted (Gio: "dopo l'inserimento della mail si apre una pagina che dice di guardare la casella di posta… click al link si atterra e bisogna ancora cliccare conferma. troppa roba!") · Figma: no frame yet for the sent / error state of `footer newsletter` or for the confirm page (design/open.md)

**Context.** Buttondown's embed form (0076) costs the visitor: leave the site for Buttondown's "check your inbox" page, open the email, click, then click Confirm on another Buttondown page. Buttondown keeps that button on purpose (email link scanners open links on their own) and cannot drop it; its redirect settings only swap its pages for ours, with the same clicks. Germany expects double opt-in (UWG §7, GDPR proof of consent), so single opt-in is not an option.
**Decision.** The site runs the double opt-in itself and Buttondown only keeps the list and sends. The FooterNewsletter form (shop footer and coming-soon page) posts to the Supabase function `newsletter`, which emails a signed link through Resend and stores nothing; the form answers in place ("Almost done: we sent a link to …"). The link lands on /newsletter/confirm/ of the site the visitor signed up on; the page posts the token, and the function adds the address to Buttondown as active (API, `type: regular`) with the proof in its metadata (sign-up time and page, confirmation time, IP). The token is in the URL fragment and is only accepted by POST, so a scanner that just opens the link confirms nothing. `site.json → newsletter.autoConfirm` true = the page confirms as it opens (one click in total); false = it shows a Confirm button (for when a scanner that runs JavaScript confirms on someone's behalf). Without JS, or if the function fails, the form goes to Buttondown's own flow (`action`), so a signup is never lost.
**Rule.** A form that collects an email answers in place on the page; consent for email marketing is always double opt-in, confirmed by a POST from a page of ours, never by the GET of a link.
**Where.** `supabase/functions/newsletter`, `apps/storefront/scripts/newsletter.js`, `pages/newsletter.js`, `build.js` and `build-coming-soon.js` (/newsletter/confirm/), `packages/ui` FooterNewsletter (`endpoint`, status line, honeypot), `site.json → newsletter`, Privacy texts (Resend, Supabase, what Buttondown keeps).
