# 0044 — The bag is a page; one line per copy; adding does not hold

`2026-11-10` · accepted (Gio) · Figma: page `Bag` (2169:6159) → `bag item`, `bag summary`, Bag - desktop / mobile (filled, just sold, empty)

**Context.** Copies are unique (a listing is one copy), Stripe Checkout is hosted (shipping by zone and tax are computed there) and the 30 min hold starts only when checkout starts (`_context/backend.md`). The bag needed a home that also works as Stripe's cancel URL and as the place that shows "just sold".
**Decision.** The bag is a page, `/bag/`, not a drawer. One line = one copy: no quantity, only Remove. Adding to the bag does not reserve the copy, so a line can become unavailable (`bag item` status=unavailable: `notice` "Just sold" / "Reserved", no price, samples stay) and is left out of the subtotal; Checkout buys the available lines. The summary shows the subtotal only, shipping "Calculated at checkout", no tax line (Q5b), EUR only. The header Bag shows the count in brackets as its suffix on every page, "(0)" included, and is `state=active` on /bag/ ([0045](0045-button-active-is-the-current-page.md)); no section tab is active there. The bag lives in the browser (localStorage, listing ids); the static page carries a hidden line per sale listing and `site.js` shows the visitor's.
**Rule.** Never show a quantity for a copy. Never present the bag as a reservation: the hold belongs to checkout. A line whose listing is no longer available stays visible, marked, until the visitor removes it. Totals that depend on the address (shipping, tax) are Stripe's, never the bag's.
**Where.** `packages/ui/components.js` (BagItem, BagSummary), `components.css`; `apps/storefront/pages/bag.js`, `bag.css`, `pages/shell.js` (SiteHeader, data-listing-id), `scripts/site.js` (6. Bag), `build.js`.
