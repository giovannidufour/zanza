# 0105 — Mobile ProductCard "large" on trial, with a view switch on the PLP

`2026-11-16` · trial (Gio: "vorrei testare questa versione alternativa della card mobile … dammi la possibilità di switchare vista in PLP tra la card attuale e questa") · Figma: frame `product card` 2332:7170 (page `product card`)

**Context.** Gio drew an alternative mobile card and wants to compare it with the current one (cover 96px beside the info, [0039](0039-mobile-card-image-max-96.md)) on real listings before choosing.
**Decision.** Not a new component: `ProductCard` gets `layout` = `compact` (default, today's card) | `large`, mobile only (desktop ignores it). large = two equal columns, gap `gutter-small`: cover (1:1) left; right, from the top, purchase (Button secondary small **fill**, price left-aligned, gap 8), grade lines (body/small, gap 4), samples (padding 8 top and bottom, as the Figma `details` padding-bottom); below, full width, title block (body/medium) and tags (small), gap 8. Same markup as compact, CSS grid places it; when the cover is taller than the right column, the extra height goes under the samples, never between blocks. On the PLP (also /search/ and /catalog/), mobile `PLP header` gets a switch "View: Compact | Large" (Button secondary small, prefix "View:") next to the sort; it switches every card of the list and is remembered per browser (localStorage `zanza-card-view`). Hidden without JS. **Not drawn in Figma** (design/open.md).
**Rule.** A trial variant is a prop of the component it may replace, never a second component; when the trial ends, keep one value and remove the prop and the switch.
**Where.** `packages/ui/components.js` / `.css` / `.d.ts` (ProductCard `layout`, PlpHeader `view`), `apps/storefront/pages/plp.js`, `apps/storefront/scripts/site.js` (`initCardView`), `apps/storefront/plp.css`.
