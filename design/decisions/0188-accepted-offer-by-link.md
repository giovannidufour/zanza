# 0188 — An accepted offer goes in the Bag from its email link, without signing in

`2026-11-25` · accepted (Gio, after testing 0186 on staging: "è molto macchinoso … anche questa cosa di inviare il code" → proposal "niente login per l'offerta" → "va bene") · replaces the sign-in of 0186 · Figma: FRONTEND → `Offers` (`offer pay` state = ready | expired | paid; `sign in`, `code`, `other account` removed; screen `Offer pay - desktop · ready`; notes "best offer behaviour" and "offer emails"), `Bag` (note "bag behaviour")

**Context.** 0186 asked the buyer to sign in with the offer's email (6-digit code) so the link could not be shared. In practice it was too many steps for one record: open the email, open the link, ask a code, open another email, type it. A password account was considered and dropped: more screens (sign up, sign in, reset), the email still has to be confirmed, "lost password" support and credential risks, for no less friction.
**Decision.**
- The email link is the proof: only the offer's inbox gets it. Opening `/offer/pay/?t=…` reads the offer (function `offer`, pay-info); ready → the record goes in the Bag at the offer price, the Bag keeps the link's token (`localStorage` `highmuzik-offers`, by listing id) and the page opens the Bag with the toast "Your offer is in your bag, at the price we agreed." If the token can't be kept (no storage), the page stays on `ready` ("In your Bag at your price" + Go to your Bag).
- The Bag prices the offer line from its token (pay-info: ready / expired / paid / closed), as in 0186 (offer note, EXPIRED, SOLD OUT, a paid offer leaves the Bag). A Best offer line without a token in this browser reads RESERVED with "Your offer: open the link in its email to buy it at your price." The line lives in that browser only; opening the link elsewhere brings it there.
- Checkout sends the tokens (`offers`); create-checkout takes the offer's amount only for a copy Reserved for an accepted, unexpired offer with that token (`checkout_items(ids, tokens)`) and gives Stripe the offer's email (`customer_email`, shown fixed), so receipt and order go to the offer's inbox. No minimum order with an offer (0186).
- No accounts for offers: the Auth hook is back to 0101 (an account only for an email with an order); `customer_offers()` is dropped.
- Accept email: "Open this link and it goes in your Bag at that price: {link}."
- Accepted risk: a forwarded link lets someone else pay the agreed price. Gio is paid the agreed price anyway.
**Rule.** Don't add a sign-in to prove an email when the link was sent to that email: the link is the proof. Ask for an account only when the buyer gets something lasting from it (orders, history).
