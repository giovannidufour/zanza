# 0200 — Admin Take photo: a phone photo of a 7" record made into the Scanner's two crops

`2026-11-27` · proposed · Figma: page `Crate photo` (2612:4308, backend mode): components `crate photo head`, `side switch`, `camera viewer`, `crop preview`, `turn bar`, `check line` (section 2612:4309); screens camera / camera no sheet / result / result fallback / added (toast); `drop zone` (2120:1270) gets the boolean `take photo` (on only in the Images zones of Create)

**Context.** Gio (2026-11-27), "Bargain crate": cheap records listed with a phone photo instead of a scan, but the photos must look as homogeneous as the Scanner's (0153): framing, automatic crop and tilt fix, centring on the spindle hole, even light, turning by drag with a live preview on the crops. He chose an admin page on the phone (iPhone 13 mini) over a photo mode in the Mac Scanner. An A4 sheet with references is a plus, not required, and shows nothing inside the crops. The Full photo is kept online with the SKU but not shown in the shop for now.
**Decision.**
- **Entry**: Create / Edit listing → Images → `Take photo` next to Browse image (DropZone `onTakePhoto`). It covers the form (the form stays mounted, hidden: unsaved edits and uploads go on); the phone's Back returns to the form.
- **Camera**: live back camera at full resolution (3024 × 4032 on the iPhone 13 mini), 3:4 viewer with an on-screen guide that is never in the photo (record edge 7", label crop 3.8", the 4 sheet markers); the status pill says whether the sheet is in view ("Sheet found" / "No sheet: fill the circle with the record"). `From library` takes a photo already made.
- **Automatic** (`packages/crate-photo`, pure JS, ported from the tested prototype): sheet markers → exact homography; no sheet → the record outline made a circle; centre = the spindle hole (7.24 or 38.2 mm), else the outline; light = a plane per channel fitted on the paper around the record, divided out (paper → 240). Two crops of 1600 px, Full 180 mm and Label 96.52 mm, both centred on the hole.
- **Turn**: drag either crop (both turn together, live at 400 px, 1600 on release), arrow keys 1° (Shift 10°), 90°, Reset; the angle shows in the turn bar.
- **Check lines** say what was applied (done) or the fallback used (warning): tilt, centre, light, plus a warning when the Label crop is enlarged more than ~10 % (shoot closer) or the record sits off the sheet's centre.
- **Add to listing** (was "Save to draft" in the first Figma draft; Figma updated): the Label becomes photo 1 (Label A) or 2 (Label B), the photos already there move down; the Full goes to R2 `originals/` and `listings.original_keys` (migration 0045), never read by the shop. Both are added to the form like any upload: **saving the listing keeps them** (the toast says so). Each can be unticked.
**Rule.** Photos that must match the Scanner's go through `packages/crate-photo` (same mm crops as 0153, centred on the hole); originals that are kept but not shown live in `original_keys`, never in `photo_keys`.
**Open.** (1) Colour match to the Scanner (contrast, blacks, saturation): Gio sends phone/scan pairs; proposal = a grey scale printed on the sheet outside the Full crop · (2) desktop layout not in Figma (code: the mobile column, max 560) · (3) the Full photos are not visible or removable in the admin; taking a side twice keeps both Fulls · (4) a large angle shows the table in the Full corners.
Figma fixes made with this decision: `icon-warning-circle` dot had no fill (now filled like its strokes); the Keep checkboxes overflowed 343 (now fill width).
