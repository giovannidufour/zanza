# 0186 — An accepted offer goes in the Bag, after signing in with the offer's email

`2026-11-25` · accepted (Gio: "mettiamo caso che l'utente voglia comprare il disco, però come parte di un ordine più grande" → "e se chiedessi all'utente di loggarsi con la stessa mail? così il link non può essere condiviso" → "vai, fai tutto") · replaces the single-record pay page of 0158 · Figma: FRONTEND → `Offers` (`offer pay` state = sign in | code | other account | expired | paid; `ready` removed; screens `Offer pay - desktop · sign in` / `· code`; notes "best offer behaviour" and "offer emails"), `Bag` (`bag item` status = offer | offer expired, text property `offer note`; screens `Bag - desktop / mobile · with accepted offer`; note "bag behaviour")

**Context.** Since 0158 an accepted offer had its own Stripe Checkout (`/offer/pay/?t=…`): one record, one shipping. A buyer who wanted that record and others paid twice and got two parcels. The link in the email also worked for anyone who had it.
**Decision.**
- The email link signs the buyer in with **the offer's email** (the account's 6-digit code, 0101; the email is fixed on the page, no field) and puts the record in the Bag at the offer price; the page then opens the Bag ("Your offer is in your bag, at the price we agreed."). Already signed in with that email: straight to the Bag. Signed in with another email: "Signed in with another email" + Sign out.
- The copy stays **Reserved** for the buyer until the 48 h end (0158), so nobody else can buy it; first to pay wins (0116) still holds for the other records of the Bag.
- Bag line (`bag item` status=offer): the offer's amount as price, `offer note` "Your offer · held until {Wed 25 Nov, 16:30}" (body/small, text/moderate) above the name. Expired (48 h over, not paid): status=offer expired, as sold (0064) with "EXPIRED" + struck amount, "Your offer expired on …", out of the totals, and the summary says "1 offer has expired and won't be in this order." Sold or closed meanwhile: SOLD OUT as any line. Paid (another tab or device): leaves the Bag.
- The offer line is priced from the account (`customer_offers()`), so it shows wherever the buyer is signed in; the rest of the Bag stays in the browser (0044). Not signed in: the line reads RESERVED with "Your offer: sign in to buy it at your price." (link to the account). Remove takes it out of this Bag only; the hold stays until it ends and the email link brings it back.
- Checkout = the Bag's (create-checkout) with the account's token: the server takes the offer's amount only for a copy Reserved for an accepted, unexpired offer of that email, and remembers the Stripe session on the offer so `checkout_claim` (0034) lets it buy the copy. A Bag with an accepted offer has **no minimum order** (an offer never had one, 0158).
- Account: an email with an accepted (or paid) offer can get an account too (Auth hook), not only one with an order (0101). Still no empty accounts.
- Emails: "{record}: you can buy it now" now says "Sign in with this email and it goes in your Bag at that price: {link}. You can add other records and pay once, with one shipping." The offer form and the confirmation page say the record goes in the Bag, not "payment link".
**Rule.** A price agreed with one buyer is tied to that buyer's verified email, never to a link: show it only to the signed-in account and let the server re-check it at checkout. Anything that changes what the buyer pays gets its own words on the Bag line and above Checkout.
**Where.** `supabase/migrations/20261125000000_offers_in_bag.sql` (0040: `offer_pay_info` + email and listing, `customer_offers`, `checkout_items(ids, email)`, `checkout_open_offers`, Auth hook) · Edge Functions `create-checkout` (Authorization header, offers, no minimum), `offer` (pay-info returns email + listing; `pay` kept but unused), `offer-decide` (new accept email) · `packages/ui` `BagItem` (`status` offer | offer-expired, `offerNote`) · `apps/storefront/pages/offer.js` (`OfferPay`), `pages/bag.js` (Best offer copies get a hidden line), `build.js`, `scripts/site.js` (`initOfferPay`, `readBagOffers`, `offerLine`, checkout token, minimum), `scripts/account.js` (`sendCode`, `verifyCode`, `signOut`, `rpc`), `trade.css`.
