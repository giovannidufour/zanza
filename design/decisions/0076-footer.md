# 0076 — Site footer: newsletter slot, the menu's links, legal and copyright

`2026-11-14` · accepted (Gio: "minimal e in linea col sito"; first proposal by Claude, merged on his go) · Figma: page `footer` → `footer` (device=desktop | mobile), `footer link list`, `footer newsletter`, `footer legal`

**Context.** The shop had no footer: the legal links (needed in Germany on every page) and the info pages were reachable only from the mobile menu, and the newsletter signup (Buttondown, chosen 2026-11-12) needed a home.
**Decision.** One `<footer>` after `<main>` on every shop page, built only from what the site already uses: surface/accent, a 1px `border divider/strong` on top (the header's border, mirrored), body/small text in text/strong, paddings `medium-small`, gaps `small` / `medium-small`. No titles over the columns. Content: newsletter (Input text field + secondary Button + note ending with an underlined Privacy link) · For sale, For trade · About, How trading works, Grading, Shipping, Contact (the mobile menu's links) · Gio's Instagram and Discogs · "© year store name" + Impressum, Privacy, Terms, Withdrawal. Desktop: newsletter and links side by side; mobile: stacked, links in two columns, legal above the copyright. Booleans `newsletter` and `social` (ON in the component). The signup stays off in code until `site.json` → `newsletter.action` is set, i.e. until the Privacy page names Buttondown.
**Rule.** Footer and mobile menu list the same pages: a page added to one is added to the other. Footer links are 16px lines with 8px between them (24px pitch, WCAG 2.5.8 by spacing); a link inside running text in the footer is underlined (1.4.1). The store name comes from `site.json`, never typed.
**Where.** `packages/ui/components.js` (`Footer`, `FooterLinks`, `FooterNewsletter`), `components.css`, `components.d.ts`, `docs/Footer/`; `apps/storefront/pages/shell.js` (`SiteFooter`, shared `LEGAL_LINKS`), `build.js` (footer as the last child of `.zz-page`), `base.css` (`.zz-page` column, footer at the bottom of short pages), `site.json` → `newsletter`.
