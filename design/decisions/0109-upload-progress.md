# 0109 — Upload progress: a ProgressBar on every file while it converts and uploads

`2026-11-17` · accepted · Figma: none yet. The `file item` row and `bulk record` have no uploading state drawn (design/open.md); this reuses `progress bar` (0091)

**Context.** Converting a 320 kbps sample to 128 (0108) takes ~10 s for 4 minutes of audio, then the upload runs. The row only said "· uploading…", and when the conversion did not happen nothing said so. Gio: "when the samples are uploading or converting, give me a progress bar".
**Decision.** Real progress, not an estimate: a sample that is converted counts 0–60 % while encoding (frames encoded) and 60–100 % while uploading (bytes sent, XHR upload progress: fetch has none); anything else 0–100 % uploading (a photo: its 1600 file; the small copies follow). Create: the file row says "· converting to 128 kbps…" or "· uploading…" and shows a `ProgressBar` under the row (FileItem children, gap 16), gone when done. Bulk: `BulkRecord` takes `uploadProgress` (the mean of its files still going up, waiting ones count 0) and shows the same bar under the files line; the files line says "converting N to 128 kbps · uploading N". A high-bitrate sample that goes up as it is (no decoder, no encoder) is never silent: Create shows it in the message line, Bulk as a warning of the record.
**Rule.** Any wait over ~2 s on a file shows a ProgressBar with the phase in words; when the progress is measurable it is the real one (0091 is for waits with none). A step that silently falls back must say so.
**Where.** `apps/admin/admin.js` (putWithProgress, upload, uploadFile onState / onNote, toSampleMp3 onConvert, fileItem, uploadProgressOf, filesText, bulkWarnings), `packages/ui/components.js` + `.d.ts` (BulkRecord `uploadProgress`).
