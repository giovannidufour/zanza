# 0124 — Record images sit on surface/media (4% black), not light blue

`2026-11-22` · accepted (Gio: "preferirei un nero con opacità bassissima. aggiorna sia figma che codice") · amends [0123](0123-admin-list-thumbnails.md) · Figma: new variable `color` → `surface/media` (alias `black-a4`, frontend and backend, scopes frame/shape fill); bound to COMPONENTS → `media` → `product image` (3:1006) and to the `thumb` rectangles of `release option` / `chosen release` (page Trade)

**Context.** Before a record photo loads (or when a record has none), its square showed `surface/support-1`: light blue in the shop, a flash of colour on every card, tile and thumbnail. Gio wants an almost invisible black instead.
**Decision.** New semantic token `surface-media` = `black-a4` in every mode: the fill behind every record image (cover, photo, thumbnail). It replaces `surface-support-1` on ProductCard image, ProductTile photo, FileList thumbnail, release option thumb, `Thumbnail` (0123); it replaces `surface-subtle` (same value, no visual change) on the product return card cover and the admin Bulk photo. `surface-support-1` stays the tag / chip background only. A dedicated token, not `surface-subtle`, because that one means "locked / drop zone" and the two may part ways (e.g. a loading skeleton). Being translucent it shows the ground: on acid green it reads as a darker green, on white / cream as a light grey; on a hovered ProductCard (surface/hover, also 4%) the square stacks to ~8%.
**Rule.** Any box that holds a record image gets `surface-media` as its background; never a hue, never a hardcoded grey.
**Where.** `packages/ui/tokens.json` (`surface-media`), `packages/ui/components.css`, `apps/admin/admin.css`, `components.d.ts` / docs.
