# 0185 — The old illustration is the ZANZA logo, and the Mac app icon

`2026-11-25` · accepted (Gio: "l'iconcina precedente deve essere l'icona / logo di zanza. E usala per lo scanner perché lo scanner non è HIGHMUZIK") · amends 0183 (Scanner icon) · Figma: `zanza logo` (2558:14, was `logo illustration`), `favicon` → `environment=scanner`

**Context.** 0183 gave every square use, the Mac app icon included, the HIGHMUZIK `logo mark` (HM) and kept the old shop illustration unused. 0184 then made the Mac app a Zanza platform tool (ZANZA), so an HM icon on it mixed the store's brand into the platform.
**Decision.** The old illustration is the **ZANZA logo**, the platform's mark. The Mac app icon (`apps/scanner/AppIcon.icns`, Figma `favicon` variant `environment=scanner`) is the ZANZA logo on the acid-green ground in the macOS rounded square: the same icon the app had before 0183. Shop, admin and coming-soon favicons stay HM (0183).
**Rule.** Platform tools (Mac app and any future internal tool) use `zanza logo`; anything a customer sees uses `logo` / `logo mark` (HIGHMUZIK). Never mix the two.
**Where.** `apps/scanner/AppIcon.icns`, `apps/scanner/README.md` (Icon); `apps/storefront/public/logo.png` is the ZANZA logo's file.
