# 0085 — Home page: Just in, For trade, Genres, About; the store name lit as the current page

`2026-11-14` · accepted (Gio: sections "ok su tutto", design "vai costruisci, poi merge"; store name lit: Gio's request) · Figma: page `Home` (2285:4045): Home - desktop / mobile, components section (`section head`, `genre links`, `home about`); page `header` → `store name` (state=default | active)

**Context.** "/" redirected to /sale/. The site is personal (Gio as DJ and collector): the home has to sell first, then give the way in to trade and a reason to trust the shop. No photos or copy exist for a hero.
**Decision.** Desktop mirrors the PLP grid: a 3-column side (where the filters are) with **Genres** (the genres of the available sale listings, A–Z, Tag size=medium links to `/sale/?genre=`) and **About** (Gio's bio from `site.json` → owner, his Discogs card, link to /about/); a 9-column main with **Just in** (the 8 newest sale listings, available or reserved, as product cards; "Play all" queues their samples in the site player, "See all" → /sale/) and **For trade** (the 4 newest available trade listings, one line + How trading works, "See all" → /trade/; hidden when there are none). Mobile: one column, Just in, For trade, Genres, About. The h1 is the store name, visually hidden. In the header, the store name is lit on the home page like the tab of the current section (`tab/background/active`, `tab/border/active`, dashed border kept; `aria-current="page"`). Left out on purpose: hero/banner, gigs and mixes (0068 amendment), timed drops (phase 2), bestsellers (one copy per record), an Instagram embed (third-party cookies).
**Rule.** A home block is a `section head` (divider + title + actions) and existing components; the first block under the header has no divider. The home lists only what can be bought or offered for now (no sold copies). Section actions are secondary Buttons.
**Where.** `packages/ui` (`SectionHead`, `GenreLinks`, `HomeAbout`, Header `homeCurrent`; `docs/Home/`), `apps/storefront/pages/home.js`, `home.css`, `pages/shell.js` (SiteHeader `current: 'home'`), `build.js` (`dist/index.html`), `scripts/site.js` (`data-play-all`).

**Amended 2026-11-15 (Gio).** Just in mixes sale and trade: the 8 newest listings of both sections (sale available or reserved, trade available), told apart only by the card's own button ("Add to bag" / "Make an offer") and price line ("Trade only"). The For trade section and the `trade intro` component are gone, and so is Just in's "See all" (it led to /sale/ only): the full lists are the header tabs. Mobile order: Genres on top (no divider), then Just in, then About; desktop unchanged (Genres and About in the side column).
