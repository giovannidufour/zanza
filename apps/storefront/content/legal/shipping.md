# Shipping

If your country is not listed, write to me: sometimes it can be arranged.

## What it costs

The cost depends on your country and on how many records, and which formats, you buy: they decide the package and its weight. The bag shows the shipping cost for your country before you go to checkout, and you always see the total before paying.

## When it arrives

I ship within [[p:3 working days]] after your payment. Usual delivery times after shipping: [[p:Germany 1–3 working days, EU 3–7, rest of the world 7–21]].

## Customs

Outside the EU, import duties and taxes of the destination country may apply. They are paid by you to the carrier or the customs office.

## Payment

You pay at checkout with one of the methods offered there (handled by Stripe).

## Returns and damage

You can return records within 14 days: see [Withdrawal](/withdrawal/). If a parcel arrives damaged, keep the packaging and write to me with photos.
