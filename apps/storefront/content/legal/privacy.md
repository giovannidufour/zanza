## What I process

- **Visiting the site:** Cloudflare delivers pages, photos and samples and sees your IP address and browser; short security logs. Art. 6(1)(f): a secure site.
- **In your browser:** your bag and, if you sign in, your sign-in. Nothing else is stored on your device.
- **Orders:** Stripe takes the payment; I get your name, address, email and order, stored in the shop database (Supabase, EU) and given to the carrier. Art. 6(1)(b) and (c); kept as long as tax and civil law require.
- **Account:** your email and a sign-in code. Art. 6(1)(b). Delete it any time under Account → Details.
- **Notify me:** your email, until that one email is sent or the record is gone. Art. 6(1)(b).
- **Contact form{{#trade}} and trade offers{{/trade}}:** what you write, sent to my mailbox (Google), kept while we talk. Art. 6(1)(b).{{#trade}} The Discogs search in the form loads cover images from Discogs.{{/trade}}
- **Newsletter:** your email, with time, page and IP address of sign-up as proof of consent, at Buttondown, until you unsubscribe (link in every email). Art. 6(1)(a).

Emails from the shop go out through Resend. Cloudflare, Resend, Buttondown, Google{{#trade}}, Discogs{{/trade}} and Supabase are US companies: transfers are covered by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Giving me data is optional, but without it I can't ship an order or answer you.

## Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection (Art. 15–18, 20, 21 GDPR), and withdrawing consent at any time: write to {{legal.email}}. You can also complain to a data protection authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
