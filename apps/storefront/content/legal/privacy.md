No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no cookies. I only use what the shop needs and what you send me.

## What, and with whom

- **Visiting the site:** hosted by Cloudflare (USA), which sees your IP address to deliver pages and keeps short security logs. Photos and samples come from Cloudflare too. Fonts are served by the site itself.
- **Availability and "Notify me":** the page checks with the shop database (Supabase, Frankfurt) whether a record is still there. If you ask to be told when a reserved record is back, your email is kept until that one email is sent (through Resend, USA) or the record is gone, then deleted.
- **Your bag** is saved only in your own browser.
- **Trade offers** reach me by email (through Resend) with what you wrote in the form; nothing is stored in a database. The Discogs search in the form goes through the shop's server; the small cover images load from Discogs.
- **Newsletter:** Buttondown (USA) keeps your email after you confirm it (double opt-in). Unsubscribe with the link in every email.
- **Orders:** Stripe (Ireland) handles payment on its own page; I get your name, address, email and what you bought, give your address to the carrier, and keep order records as long as tax law requires.

US providers are covered by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Why (GDPR)

Running the site securely and showing correct availability: Art. 6(1)(f). Things you ask for (a reminder, a trade, an order): Art. 6(1)(b). Newsletter: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a). Tax records: Art. 6(1)(c).

## Your rights

You can ask me to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or move it, and object to its use (Art. 15–21 GDPR): write to {{legal.email}}. You can also complain to a data protection authority.
