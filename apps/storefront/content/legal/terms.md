# Terms

Private sales: I am a private person selling records from my own collection, not a shop.

- **The records** are used, one copy each, sold as described: grading, notes and photos of that copy.
- **Buying:** price and shipping are shown in the bag. The sale is agreed when your payment goes through and I confirm it by email.
- **No returns, no warranty:** as a private sale there is no right of withdrawal and no warranty, except that the record must be as described. If it is not, write to me with photos.
- **Shipping:** see [Shipping](/shipping/). Outside the EU, import duties are paid by you.
{{#trade}}- **Trades** are agreed one by one by email; nothing is agreed until we both say yes.
{{/trade}}- German law applies.
