# Terms

These are private sales: I am a private person selling records from my own collection, not a shop.

- **The records** are used, one copy each, described with my [grading](/grading/), notes and photos of that copy. What is described is what you get.
- **Buying:** the price and shipping are shown in the bag; the sale is agreed when your payment (through Stripe) goes through and I confirm it by email.
- **No returns, no warranty:** as a private sale there is no right of withdrawal and no warranty, except that the record must be as described. If it is not, write to me with photos and we sort it out.
- **Shipping:** see [Shipping](/shipping/). Outside the EU, import duties are paid by you.
- **Trades** are agreed one by one by email; nothing is agreed until we both say yes.
- German law applies.
